# Presenter notes: Survive the Cash Crunch

Press **N** on the host screen at any time to see a short note for the current step (only you see it). This page is the longer version to read once before the show.

---

## While people join (about 2 minutes)

> "Phones out, please. Scan the code. No app, no sign-up. **Every one of you now owns a cloud kitchen with ₹5 lakh in the bank.**"

Read a few funny kitchen names out loud as they pop up.

## The 45-second intro

> "Here's how it works. Every week there's a quick question. **Right answers put cash into your kitchen, and the faster you answer, the more you get. Wrong answers cost you ₹10,000.**
>
> Then you run your kitchen for the week: discount, ads, ingredients, staff. Everyone gets the same news and the same luck. Only your choices are different.
>
> There's a clock at the top. When it hits zero, **the kitchen with the most cash wins**, and we'll open up the winner's balance sheet on screen to see *how* they did it."

Press **Space**. The **briefing** comes up on the projector and every phone. The clock is still paused.

## The briefing (about 60 seconds)

Walk through the six cards. Stress two of them:
> "ZipEats pays you **a week late**. Until then it's a *receivable*, money that's yours but isn't in the bank."
> "Rent and staff cost **₹58,000 every week**, even if nobody orders."

Then: "Every question today is an accounting question. Ready?" Press **Space** to start the clock.

---

## Each week

**Headline (10s).** Read it out loud. If it's an event, read the effect.

**Question (20s, ends as soon as everyone has answered).** Read the question. Build tension in the last seconds.

**Answer.** Read the 💡 line. **That's the lesson for the week.** One sentence, then move on.

**Your kitchen (20s, ends as soon as everyone has locked in).**
> "Run YOUR kitchen. If you're happy with last week, tap 'Same as last week'."

**Results.** Point at the big number:
> "Look: most of you sold MORE this week, and most of you have LESS cash. Where did it go?"

Then point at the top 5 and ask the leader: *"What did you do differently?"*

### Special moments
- **Payout delay (the cash trap):** the question that week is "How much will ZipEats pay you?" (answer: nothing). Let it land.
- **A phone says "Your kitchen has closed":** they ran out of cash twice. They can still answer questions to pay off their bills.
- **Clock under a minute:** it turns red. The current week still finishes.
- Need more time? **C** adds a minute to the game clock. **+** adds 15 seconds to the current question.

---

## The ending: the winner's balance sheet (3 screens, Space to move through them)

1. **Title + balance sheet.**
   > "Congratulations to [winner]! Let's open their books."

   Walk through the three numbered callouts. Point at the ✓:
   > "What the kitchen has always equals what it owes plus the owner's share. That's a balance sheet."
2. **Chart + "What a CFO would say".** Read the two lines slowly.
3. **Same weeks, played the careful way + how the room ended.** Point at how many kitchens closed, and at the most common ending.

## The closing (30 seconds)

> "The winner wasn't the kitchen with the most orders. It was the one that kept its cash.
>
> **Margins**: how much you keep from each order.
> **Payout delays**: sales today, cash next week.
> **Fixed costs**: rent doesn't care how busy you were.
>
> Growth is not cash."

Optional: press **Play again with the same room**. Events reshuffle, and people play very differently the second time.
